About the Designer

I’m Alif, a web designer who enjoys shaping ideas into clear, responsive interfaces. I focus on layout, visual hierarchy, usability, and the details that make a website feel considered.

I’m continuing to develop my frontend skills through hands-on projects, testing designs in real browsers rather than leaving them locked in design files. My goal is simple: craft web experiences that communicate clearly, load quickly, and feel dependable to everyone who uses them.
