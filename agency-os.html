// 01. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW

THE MISSION

Agency OS is a workspace concept designed to help TikTok Shop agencies manage creators, brand campaigns, LIVE studio schedules, and commission payouts in one place.

The goal was simple: make complex daily operations easier to organize, monitor, and scale without cross-platform friction. By anchoring every operational entity into a single synchronized database, agencies transition from fire-fighting daily logistical chaos to running predictable, high-velocity broadcast campaigns.

// 02. THE CORE PROBLEM — THE FRAGMENTED STACK

SILOED DISRUPTION

Managing an agency involves far more than running campaigns. Creator information, brand briefs, content deadlines, LIVE schedules, and commission records frequently end up scattered across disconnected spreadsheets and siloed communication tools.

This separation creates unnecessary handoffs, makes end-to-end campaign progress difficult to track, and introduces high operational friction during monthly payout reconciliations.
